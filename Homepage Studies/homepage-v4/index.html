01 / Rooted in Colorado

A Colorado Glass Company Serving Homes and Businesses Since 1984

Ken Caryl Glass Inc. is a full-service glass company serving Littleton, Colorado Springs, and nearby communities. Since 1984, Ken Caryl Glass has provided residential, commercial, and custom glass services, from window repairs and shower enclosures to storefronts and architectural glazing. With two Colorado locations, we help homeowners, businesses, and contractors find the right glass solutions.

Ken Caryl Glass started in August 1984 with just two people and one vehicle. More than four decades later, we've grown into a full-service glass company with locations in Littleton and Colorado Springs.

Our work ranges from replacing cracked windowpanes and cutting custom tabletops to installing shower enclosures, commercial entrances, and architectural glass systems. Each project brings its own requirements, and the details matter.

A storefront entrance needs durable materials and hardware that can handle daily use. Frameless shower enclosures call for precise measurements and secure mounting, while custom glass pieces may need specific thicknesses and edge finishes. We consider these details when helping customers plan their projects.

05 / Comfort through glass

3M Window Film & Specialty Glass Solutions

Colorado's strong sunshine can bring unwanted heat, glare, and UV exposure through windows. Architectural window film helps address these concerns in homes, offices, and commercial spaces. Ken Caryl Glass is a 3M Authorized Window Film Dealer with Certified 3M Installers. We offer genuine 3M building window films, including solar-control, UV-protection, safety, and security options.

From reducing afternoon glare to helping hold broken glass fragments together, different films serve different purposes. We help customers choose suitable options based on their existing glass and specific needs.

24-Hour Emergency Board-Up Services

A shattered storefront window or broken glass entrance can leave your property exposed to weather, trespassers, and further damage. Securing the opening quickly matters, especially when the glass can't be replaced right away.

Ken Caryl Glass offers 24-hour emergency board-up services for homes and commercial properties. We help temporarily secure damaged openings while permanent glass repair or replacement is arranged. Contact your nearest Ken Caryl Glass office for assistance.
