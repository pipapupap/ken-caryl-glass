Residential Glass Assessment and Installation

Residential glass repair, replacement, and installation requirements depend on the existing glass, dimensions, surrounding components, and intended application.

Glass Repair or Glass-Only Replacement

Glass-only replacement may be possible when the existing window or door frame, sash, and related components remain suitable for continued use.

Broken glass and failed insulated glass units generally require replacement of the affected glass or sealed unit. Damaged or incompatible surrounding components may require additional replacement work.

Glass Measurement, Ordering, and Installation

Residential glass replacement and custom installations generally involve three stages:

1. Assessment and Measurement

The existing glass, opening dimensions, frame condition, hardware, and relevant components are evaluated.

2. Glass Selection and Preparation

Glass specifications are confirmed, and replacement materials are ordered or fabricated according to project requirements.

3. Installation

Installation is scheduled according to material availability, fabrication requirements, and site conditions.

Project timelines depend on the type of glass, required materials, and installation conditions.
